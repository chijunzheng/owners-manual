Residential Tenancy Agreement

Parties and Rental Unit

This Residential Tenancy Agreement is made between Pat Morgan (the "Landlord") and Sam Rivera (the "Tenant") for the residential unit known as Suite 1204, Harbourview Terrace, 12 Fictional Crescent, Toronto, Ontario (the "Unit").

The rental unit is a unit in a condominium. The Tenant acknowledges that the Unit is governed by a condominium corporation and agrees to comply with the declaration, by-laws and rules of the corporation, as amended from time to time, copies of which have been provided to the Tenant by the Landlord.

The tenancy is for a fixed term of one year commencing on the first day of the month and is subject to the Residential Tenancies Act, 2006.

Rent

The Tenant shall pay rent of $2,000.00 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each month, without deduction, set-off or abatement, to the Landlord at the address designated by the Landlord for that purpose.

Rent not received by the Landlord on the day it is due shall be considered late, and the Tenant shall pay any lawful administration charge for a cheque returned by a financial institution.

Deposits

First and Last Months' Rent

On signing this Agreement the Tenant shall pay to the Landlord a deposit equal to the first and last months' rent, in the total amount of $4,000.00, which the Landlord shall hold in trust as security for the performance of the Tenant's obligations under this Agreement. The deposit shall bear no interest, and no interest shall be earned, received or payable on it for the duration of the tenancy.

Key Deposit

The Tenant shall pay a key deposit of $300.00 for the keys, entry fobs and garage remote issued to the Tenant. The key deposit shall be refunded to the Tenant after the Tenant vacates the Unit and returns it in its original condition and cleanliness, reasonable allowance for the cost of any cleaning or repair being deducted by the Landlord.

Pets

No pets of any kind are permitted in the Unit. The keeping of any pet in the Unit shall constitute a substantial breach of this Agreement.

Maintenance and Repairs

The Tenant is responsible for all damage and repairs of any kind to the Unit and its contents arising during the tenancy, whatever the cause, and shall keep the Unit in a good state of repair at the Tenant's own expense.

The Tenant shall pay the first $100.00 of the cost of every repair to the fixtures and appliances in the Unit, and the Landlord shall be responsible only for the portion of any such repair that exceeds that amount.

Renewal

If the Tenant does not provide the Landlord with sixty days' written notice of the Tenant's intention to renew before the end of the term, the Landlord may show the Unit to prospective tenants and may make arrangements to re-let the Unit at the end of the term.

Schedule A — Additional Rent Arrangements

The Tenant voluntarily offers, as an accommodation to the Landlord and of the Tenant's own free will, to prepay two additional months of rent on the move-in date. The Landlord shall apply the prepaid amounts to the rent owing for the tenth and eleventh months of the term, and the Tenant agrees that no rent payment shall be required in those two months.

Additional Terms

Smoking is not permitted anywhere in the Unit or in the common areas of the building. The Tenant shall not smoke, and shall not permit any occupant or guest to smoke, within the Unit.

The Tenant shall obtain and maintain, for the duration of the tenancy, a policy of tenant's liability insurance, and shall provide the Landlord with proof of that insurance on request.

The Tenant shall not change, add to or re-key the locks on the Unit without the prior written consent of the Landlord, and shall provide the Landlord with a key to any lock installed with such consent.

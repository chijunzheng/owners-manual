Condominium Unit Owner's Policy

Declarations

Meridian Assurance Company (the "Insurer") agrees to provide the insurance described in this policy to the Insured named below, in return for payment of the premium and subject to all of the terms, conditions, and exclusions that follow. This is a contract of personal insurance covering a unit owner's interest in a residential condominium unit.

Insured: the unit owner named on the application, in respect of Unit 1108, Toronto Standard Condominium Corporation No. 9000 ("Harbourview Terrace"), 12 Fictional Crescent, Toronto, Ontario.

Policy Period: 1 January 2026 to 1 January 2027, both days at 12:01 a.m. Standard Time at the address of the Insured. Broker: Northgate Insurance Brokers.

This policy is intended to cover the personal property of the Insured and the Insured's exposure to the corporation's deductible and to liability, and to sit alongside the corporation's master policy, which insures the common elements and the standard unit. This policy does not duplicate the coverage provided by the corporation's master policy.

Coverage A — Personal Property

The Insurer insures the personal property of the Insured, being the contents, furnishings, clothing, and personal belongings usual to the occupancy of a residential unit and owned by or in the care of the Insured, against direct physical loss or damage caused by an insured peril, up to the limit shown on the application.

Insured perils for personal property include fire, smoke, theft, and the sudden and accidental escape of water from a plumbing, heating, or air-conditioning system or from a domestic appliance within the unit or the building.

Coverage B — Water Damage

The Insurer insures direct physical loss or damage to insured personal property caused by the sudden and accidental escape of water or steam from within a plumbing, heating, sprinkler, or air-conditioning system, or from a domestic water-using appliance, located in the unit or elsewhere in the building.

This coverage applies only to escape of water that is sudden and accidental. It does not apply to loss or damage caused by water that develops over a period of time through a continuous or repeated seepage or leakage, nor to loss or damage that the Insured could have prevented through reasonable maintenance.

Coverage C — Loss Assessment and Deductible

The Insurer will indemnify the Insured for an amount the Insured becomes liable to pay to the corporation as that owner's share of the corporation's master-policy deductible, where the corporation's master policy responds to an insured loss, up to the limit shown on the application.

Exclusions

This policy does not insure loss or damage caused by sewer back-up. Loss or damage caused by water or sewage that backs up or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, septic system, or eavestrough is not insured under this policy.

This policy does not insure loss or damage caused by flood, surface water, the rising of a water table, or the entry of water through a basement floor, foundation, or wall.

This policy does not insure loss or damage caused by wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, dampness of atmosphere, or the Insured's failure to maintain the unit.

This policy does not insure the structure of the unit, the common elements, or the standard unit, all of which are insured under the corporation's master policy.

No endorsement extending this policy to cover loss or damage caused by sewer back-up is attached to or forms part of this policy.

Conditions

In the event of loss or damage, the Insured shall give notice to the Insurer or the Broker as soon as practicable, protect the property from further loss, and provide a proof of loss within the time required by the policy.

This policy applies only to the interest of the Insured and does not cover the interest of the corporation or of any other person except as expressly stated.

This policy does not insure any improvement or betterment made to the unit beyond the standard unit defined in the corporation's by-laws, and no improvements or betterments coverage is provided by or attached to this policy.

Certificate of Property and Liability Insurance — Master Policy

Declarations

This certificate evidences that Meridian Assurance Company (the "Insurer") has issued Master Policy No. MAC-CP-447821 to the Named Insured for the policy period 1 January 2026 to 1 January 2027, both days at 12:01 a.m. Standard Time at the address of the Named Insured. This certificate is furnished as a matter of information only and is subject in all respects to the terms, conditions, exclusions, and endorsements of the policy itself.

Named Insured: Toronto Standard Condominium Corporation No. 9000, operating as "Harbourview Terrace", 12 Fictional Crescent, Toronto, Ontario, together with all registered unit owners as their respective rights and interests may appear.

Broker of Record: Northgate Insurance Brokers, who may be contacted for particulars of coverage, evidence of insurance, and claims reporting.

This insurance is placed to satisfy the corporation's obligation under its declaration and the Condominium Act, 1998 to obtain and maintain insurance on the common elements and the units, exclusive of improvements and betterments made by owners.

Insured Property

The Insurer agrees, subject to the limit shown below, to indemnify the Named Insured against direct physical loss of or damage to the insured property caused by an insured peril occurring during the policy period.

The property insured under this policy comprises the common elements of the corporation and the standard unit of each unit, as the term "standard unit" is defined in the by-laws of the corporation. This policy does not insure any improvement or betterment to a unit made by or on behalf of an owner beyond the standard unit so defined.

For greater certainty, the determination of what constitutes the standard unit, and therefore the boundary between property insured by the corporation under this policy and property that is the responsibility of the owner, is governed by the corporation's standard unit by-law and not by this certificate.

Limit of Insurance

The limit of insurance for direct physical loss or damage to the insured property is $50,000,000 in any one occurrence, being the blanket replacement-cost limit for the common elements and the standard units combined.

Insured Perils

This policy insures against all risks of direct physical loss or damage to the insured property except as excluded, including loss or damage caused by fire, smoke, escape of water from plumbing, heating, or sprinkler systems, and water that backs up or overflows from a sewer, drain, or sump.

Deductibles

From the amount of each claim otherwise payable, the applicable deductible shown below is first deducted. Where more than one deductible could apply to a single occurrence, the highest applicable deductible applies and applies only once.

Base property deductible: $10,000 each occurrence, applicable to all insured physical loss or damage except where a specific deductible is stated below.

Water damage and sewer back-up deductible: $25,000 each occurrence, applicable to loss or damage caused by water, including escape of water, flood, and water that backs up or overflows from a sewer, drain, sump, or eavestrough.

Sewer Back-Up Coverage

Loss or damage to the insured property caused by water that backs up or overflows from a sewer, drain, or sump is insured under this policy at the corporation level, subject to the water damage and sewer back-up deductible of $25,000 each occurrence stated above.

This coverage responds to loss of or damage to the common elements and the standard unit only. Contents, personal property, and any improvement or betterment beyond the standard unit are not insured under this policy and are the responsibility of the owner under the owner's own policy.

Deductible Responsibility

Where this policy responds to loss or damage that originated in or arose from a unit, the owner of that unit may be liable to the corporation for the applicable policy deductible in accordance with the corporation's declaration. The terms on which a deductible is charged to an owner are set out in the declaration and not in this policy, and nothing in this certificate creates, limits, or waives that obligation.

Exclusions

This policy does not insure loss or damage to any improvement or betterment made to a unit by or on behalf of an owner beyond the standard unit, including without limitation upgraded flooring, upgraded counters, and upgraded or built-in appliances installed in substitution for or in addition to the finishes comprised in the standard unit.

This policy does not insure the personal property, contents, or belongings of any owner, tenant, or occupant of a unit.

This policy does not insure loss or damage arising from wear and tear, gradual deterioration, faulty workmanship, or an owner's failure to maintain the unit.

Conditions

The insured shall report any loss or occurrence likely to give rise to a claim to the Broker of Record as soon as practicable after it becomes known to the insured.

This certificate may be cancelled or amended only in accordance with the terms of the policy, and the Insurer will endeavour to provide the Named Insured with notice of cancellation in accordance with the policy and applicable law.

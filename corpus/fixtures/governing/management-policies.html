Resident Policies — Harbourview Terrace (TSCC 9000)

The following policies are issued by the management office of Harbourview Terrace for the information and guidance of residents. These policies describe the procedures the management office follows in administering the building and are intended to assist residents in complying with the Declaration, by-laws, and rules of the Corporation. Residents with questions about these policies should contact the management office.

Move-In and Move-Out Procedures

Every resident moving into or out of the building must book the moving elevator and loading dock through the management office at least forty-eight hours in advance, and all moves must take place during the designated moving hours.

Before a move may proceed, the management office collects a move-in deposit of three hundred dollars, which is refundable to the resident after the move provided that no damage is caused to the common elements during the move, together with a non-refundable move-in administration charge of one hundred and fifty dollars to cover the cost of administering the move. These amounts are collected as common expenses and are added to the owner's account where not paid directly.

Where a key fob must be reactivated or reissued following a move, the management office collects a fob reactivation fee, which is likewise added to the owner's account and collected as common expenses.

Barbecues and Balconies

For the safety of all residents and to comply with the fire code, barbecues of every kind, including electric, propane, charcoal, and natural-gas barbecues, are not permitted on any balcony, terrace, or other exclusive-use area, and may not be stored or operated on any balcony at any time.

Balconies must be kept clean and free of clutter, and no item that could fall or be blown from a balcony may be stored upon it.

Short-Term and Transient Rentals

The Declaration of the Corporation prohibits short-term and transient rentals of units, including rentals arranged through Airbnb, VRBO, or any similar platform. Residents are reminded that units may not be advertised or rented for short-term or transient occupancy, and the management office monitors listing platforms for units within the building.

Owners intending to lease their units should contact the management office for the documentation required before a tenant takes occupancy.

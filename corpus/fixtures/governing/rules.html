Rules of Toronto Standard Condominium Corporation No. 9000 (Harbourview Terrace)

The following rules have been made by the board of directors of Toronto Standard Condominium Corporation No. 9000 pursuant to section 58 of the Condominium Act, 1998, to promote the safety, security, and welfare of the owners and of the property and assets of the Corporation, and to prevent unreasonable interference with the use and enjoyment of the common elements and of the units. These rules are in addition to the Declaration and the by-laws of the Corporation and bind all owners, occupants, and their guests.

Rule 1 — Pets

No owner or occupant shall keep any dog the weight of which exceeds ten kilograms in any unit or anywhere upon the property, and any dog exceeding that weight shall be permanently removed from the property upon written notice from the Corporation.

No dog shall be taken into or transported in any passenger elevator of the building at any time. An owner or occupant in possession of a dog shall use the designated service corridor and stairway for the entry and exit of the dog to and from the building.

Every owner and occupant who keeps a pet shall ensure that the pet is leashed or carried while upon the common elements and shall promptly clean up after the pet.

Rule 2 — Parking and Visitor Parking

No owner or occupant shall park any vehicle upon the common elements except in a parking space designated for that owner or occupant, and no person shall park in a visitor parking space except a bona fide visitor of an owner or occupant.

Where a vehicle is parked in contravention of this Rule, the owner or occupant responsible for the vehicle shall be fined the sum of one hundred dollars for each contravention, which fine shall be added to the common expenses attributable to that owner's unit and shall be collected in the same manner as common expenses.

A vehicle parked in contravention of this Rule may, in addition, be towed from the property at the expense of the owner of the vehicle.

Rule 3 — Leasing and Occupancy

No owner shall lease the owner's unit for a term of less than six months, and no unit shall be occupied under any arrangement, however described, for a period of less than six months. This Rule applies to every lease, sublease, licence, or other arrangement granting occupancy of a unit.

An owner who leases the owner's unit shall file with the management office the name of every lessee and occupant, a copy of the lease, and government-issued identification for every lessee, within thirty days of entering into the lease.

Where an owner fails to file the information and identification required by this Rule within thirty days, the lessee shall be deemed to be a trespasser upon the property, and the lessee shall not be entitled to book or use the moving elevator or to take possession of the unit until the management office has received the lessee's identification.

Rule 4 — Use of the Common Elements

No owner or occupant shall obstruct the common elements, leave any article upon the common elements, or use the common elements in a manner that interferes with the use and enjoyment of the common elements by others.

The moving elevator, loading dock, and service corridor shall be reserved in advance through the management office, and all moves into and out of the building shall take place only during the hours designated by the Corporation.

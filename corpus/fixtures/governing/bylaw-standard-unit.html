By-law No. 3 (Standard Unit) of Toronto Standard Condominium Corporation No. 9000 (Harbourview Terrace)

A by-law of Toronto Standard Condominium Corporation No. 9000 made under section 56 of the Condominium Act, 1998, to define the standard unit for the purposes of section 99 of the Act and the insurance obligations of the Corporation. This by-law was passed by the board of directors and confirmed by the owners in accordance with the Act.

Article 1 — Purpose and Application

The purpose of this by-law is to define the standard unit for each class of residential unit in the Corporation, so that the Corporation may determine the extent of its obligation to insure and to repair after damage under the Act and the Declaration. The standard unit defined in this by-law applies to every residential unit in the Corporation.

For the purposes of this by-law, the Corporation contains a single class of residential unit, and the standard unit described in this by-law is the standard unit for every residential unit.

Article 2 — Definition of the Standard Unit

The "standard unit" means a residential unit constructed and finished to the standard provided by the declarant on the registration of the Declaration, comprising the elements and finishes described in this Article, and no others.

Flooring

The standard unit includes vinyl plank flooring throughout the living areas, bedrooms, and hallways, and ceramic or porcelain tile flooring in the bathrooms, as supplied and installed by the declarant, together with the associated underlayment and baseboards of builder-grade material.

Countertops and Cabinetry

The standard unit includes laminate countertops in the kitchen and bathrooms and builder-grade cabinetry, as supplied and installed by the declarant.

Appliances

The standard unit includes the builder-supplied appliances provided to the unit on registration, being a refrigerator, a range, a range hood, a dishwasher, and an over-the-counter microwave, each of the make, model, and grade supplied by the declarant as part of the original construction.

Wall, Ceiling, and Other Finishes

The standard unit includes builder-grade interior walls and partitions, drywall, paint, interior doors and trim, standard light fixtures, plumbing fixtures, and the heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning components, each as supplied and installed by the declarant as part of the original construction of the unit.

Article 3 — Exclusions: Improvements and Betterments

Any element, finish, fixture, or appliance installed in a unit that exceeds, replaces, or upgrades the standard described in Article 2, whether installed by the declarant as an optional upgrade, by a subsequent purchaser, or by an owner, is an improvement to the unit and forms no part of the standard unit.

Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, the following are improvements and form no part of the standard unit: hardwood flooring; engineered hardwood flooring; stone, granite, quartz, or other natural or engineered stone countertops; upgraded, premium, or built-in appliances installed in substitution for or in addition to the builder-supplied appliances; custom or upgraded cabinetry; and any other upgrade installed in substitution for or in addition to an element or finish of the standard unit.

The Corporation's insurance and the Corporation's obligation to repair the standard unit after damage do not extend to any improvement described in this Article. Each owner is responsible for insuring and for repairing after damage any improvement made to the owner's unit, whether made by the owner or by any predecessor in title.
